# Devices and Software

## Installing software
Install software only from the Company Portal app. If the software you need is not listed, raise a ticket with a business reason. Approval takes up to 3 business days.

## Slow laptop
Restart your laptop first, then check that you have at least 10% free disk space. Close unused browser tabs and apps. If it is still slow after a restart, raise a ticket.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to security@novaretail.example within 1 hour. Do not wait to look for it first. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device.

## Laptop refresh
Laptops are replaced every 4 years.

## Printers
To add a printer, open Company Portal and install the printer package for your floor.
