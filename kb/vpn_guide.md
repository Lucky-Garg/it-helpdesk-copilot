# VPN Guide

## Connecting from home
1. Open the Cisco AnyConnect app on your company laptop.
2. Enter the server address vpn.novaretail.example and click Connect.
3. Sign in with your company SSO account and approve the MFA prompt on your phone.
Once connected, internal sites like the intranet and file shares work as if you were in the office.

## Troubleshooting connection errors
Error 809 usually means your home router is blocking the VPN. Try switching from Wi-Fi to a mobile hotspot, then reconnect. If the VPN connects but is slow, disconnect and reconnect to refresh the session. If the problem continues, open a ticket and include the exact error message.

## Supported devices
VPN is allowed only on company laptops and company-managed phones. Personal laptops and personal phones are not allowed to connect to the VPN.
