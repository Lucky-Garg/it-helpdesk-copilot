# Accounts, Passwords and MFA

## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. They expire every 90 days, and you cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.

## Resetting a forgotten password
Go to passwords.novaretail.example and choose "Forgot password". Verify your identity with the MFA prompt, then set a new password. If self-service does not work, call the service desk on ext 5555.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. It unlocks automatically. You only need to raise a ticket if it stays locked afterwards.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA uses the Microsoft Authenticator app. Never share your password or MFA code with anyone, including IT support staff. IT will never ask for them.

## Lost or replaced phone
If you lose your phone or get a new one, call the service desk to reset MFA. Your manager must approve the request before the reset is done.
