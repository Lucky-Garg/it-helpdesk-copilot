# Email and Collaboration

## Mailbox size
Each mailbox has a 50 GB limit. If your mailbox is full, archive old emails or empty Deleted Items. You can request a larger mailbox through a ticket with manager approval.

## Reporting phishing
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click links or open attachments. Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook. The security team reviews every report.

## Teams not loading or behaving strangely
Fully quit Teams, clear the Teams cache folder, and restart the app. If problems continue, sign out and sign back in.

## Meeting rooms
Book meeting rooms from Outlook using the Room Finder in a new meeting invite.
